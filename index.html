Hermes Personal

Hermes Personal is a private AI assistant used by its owner to organize and summarize email messages.

How it works

The application accesses Gmail messages with the owner's authorization using the Gmail read-only permission. It cannot send, delete, or modify emails.

Privacy Policy

Gmail data is accessed only to identify important messages and generate summaries. Relevant email content may be processed by an external AI provider (DeepSeek). OAuth credentials are stored locally on the owner's computer. Summaries are delivered to the owner's Telegram chat.

Gmail data is not sold or used for advertising. Access can be revoked through Google Account settings.

Terms of Service

This is a personal-use application provided without warranties. It is not offered as a public service.

Contact

For questions about this application, contact its owner through the developer contact information listed in the Google OAuth consent configuration.
